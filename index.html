— GPM

GPM = Cv × √ΔP(psi); psi = ft ÷ 2.31 = in.wc ÷ 27.68. Water at ~60°F; correct for glycol per manufacturer.

Fan laws

—
CFM ∝ RPM, SP ∝ RPM² × ρ, BHP ∝ RPM³ × ρ (ASHRAE Handbook – HVAC Systems & Equipment, Fans).

Sheave change

—
Sheave formulas Fan RPM = motor RPM × Dmotor / Dfan × (1 − slip). Slip = 1 − measured fan RPM / (motor RPM × Dm/Df) (0 if not measured).
Ratio r = new fan RPM / current fan RPM. CFM × r, SP × r², BHP × r³ (fan laws).
Mode B: required RPM = current RPM × target CFM / current CFM; Dm = Df × RPMreq / (motor RPM × (1 − slip)), or Df = Dm × motor RPM × (1 − slip) / RPMreq. Rounded to nearest 0.1" for reference — pick the nearest stocked sheave from the catalog and recheck.
BHP (if PF and eff entered) = V × A × (1.732) × PF × eff / 746.
Amps, simple TAB estimate: Anew = A × r³ (overstates change, ignores no-load current).
Amps, no-load (phasor) estimate: no-load (magnetizing) current I0 = % × FLA is treated as reactive and roughly constant; the load (in-phase) component IL = √(A² − I0²) scales with shaft power (r³); Anew = √(I0² + (IL r³)²). Ignores losses and PF/efficiency changes with load.
Belt speed (FPM) = π × Dmotor × motor RPM / 12.
Both amp values are estimates; measure amps after the change and verify they are within FLA (× SF).

Pump laws

—
GPM ∝ N, head ∝ N², BHP ∝ N³. Diameter trim uses the same exponents and is only approximate (efficiency changes with trim).

Unit conversions

Constants per NIST SP 811 (Guide for the Use of the International System of Units), Appendix B: https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811

Duct

—
Galvanized steel, ε = 0.0003 ft. Darcy-Weisbach, f from Swamee-Jain (explicit Colebrook approximation), Re = 8.56·D(in)·V(fpm)·ρ-ratio; ΔP = f·(1200/D)·VP; VP = ρ-ratio·(V/4005)²; De = 1.30(ab)^0.625/(a+b)^0.25. ASHRAE Handbook—Fundamentals, Ch. 21 (Duct Design). Uses site density.

Pipe

—
Darcy-Weisbach with Swamee-Jain f, water (no glycol). ε: steel 0.00015 ft, copper 0.000005 ft. IDs verified: Sch 40 per ASME B36.10M (https://pipedata.org/pipes/schedule-40/, https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/ansi-steel-pipes-d_305.html); Type L per ASTM B88, CDA Copper Tube Handbook Table 14.2b (https://www.copper.org/applications/plumbing/cth/technical-data/tables/cth_table2b.php). Water ν at the listed temperature: Engineering ToolBox (https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/water-dynamic-kinematic-viscosity-d_596.html).

Coil check

—
Air: sensible = 1.08·ρ-ratio·CFM·ΔT; total = 4.5·ρ-ratio·CFM·Δh, h from DB/WB at site elevation (ASHRAE psychrometrics). Water: k·GPM·ΔT, k = 500 × SG × cp (glycol data as Water tab). Cooling coils must be compared on TOTAL heat (latent included), not sensible. Within ±10% = OK (typical field criterion).
